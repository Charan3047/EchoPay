# EchoPay — Google Play listing draft

Short description:
Track UPI and bank spending privately on your device.

Full description:
EchoPay helps you understand everyday spending by organizing payment notifications into a searchable transaction history, category insights, and optional monthly budgets.

• Capture supported payment notifications after you enable Notification access.
• Categorize transactions on-device.
• Review spending history and monthly category insights.
• Set optional budgets and see progress as transactions arrive.
• Export a spreadsheet when you choose.
• Keep transaction records on your device; cloud backup is disabled.

EchoPay does not initiate payments or connect to your bank account. Notification access is optional. Automatic capture depends on the notification formats sent by your bank or payment app and may not recognize every notification. Review transactions for accuracy.

Privacy policy: publish PRIVACY_POLICY.md at a public HTTPS URL and enter that URL in Play Console.
Support email: add a real monitored support address before submission.
