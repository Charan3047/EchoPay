# EchoPay Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 1 November 2026_

EchoPay is an on-device personal expense tracker. It does not require an account.

## Information the app accesses

If you enable Android Notification access for EchoPay, the app reads notification titles and text so it can identify completed payment notifications. Notification text is processed on the device in memory. EchoPay does not save or log the original notification text. It extracts transaction details such as amount, merchant when available, transaction type, payment method, and notification time.

## How information is stored and used

Parsed transaction details, category budgets, and appearance preferences are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The included categorizer runs on the device. EchoPay does not send this information to EchoPay servers. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled for the app.

## Exports and sharing

You may choose to export transaction details to an Excel file. The file is written to the destination you select using Android's document picker. The exported file is then outside EchoPay's private storage and is managed by you and the destination you chose.

## Permissions and your choices

Notification access is optional and is controlled in Android Settings. Without it, automatic transaction capture is unavailable; you can still use other app features. You can revoke access at any time in Android Settings. Uninstalling EchoPay removes its private app data. Delete any exported spreadsheets separately using the file manager or destination app.

## Children

EchoPay is a general personal finance utility and is not designed for children under 13.

## Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when app behavior changes. For privacy questions, contact the developer using the support email shown on the EchoPay Google Play listing.
